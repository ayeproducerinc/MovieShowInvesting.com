---
name: Confirmed interest in unlisted projects
description: Policy for non-binding confirmation when a saved project later becomes unlisted.
---

An investor may sign the exact non-binding interest they previously saved even if a selected project later becomes hidden or unapproved. Keep that historical choice in their private record. A deleted project reference cannot be confirmed because the original choice can no longer be verified. Public totals must remain zero for projects that are not currently eligible for discovery, including publicly reachable unlisted project pages.

**Why:** Requiring current listing at signing strands investors with valid saved interest and no edit flow. Counting confirmed historical interest on an unapproved but reachable unlisted page would expose a misleading public signal.

**How to apply:** Treat signing as acknowledgment of the saved record, not a fresh project selection. Keep original allocations intact and apply current eligibility checks wherever public project totals are displayed.
