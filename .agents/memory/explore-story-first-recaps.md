name: Explore story-first recaps
description: Owner-approved scope for all current and future Explore project cards
---

Explore cards should show a quick story-first recap: poster, title, movie/show format, genre, stage, short story hook and View project. This applies to all existing and future projects.

No project on Explore should show a deal breakdown or project budget. This includes standard and negotiated terms; visitors see budget and deal details only after choosing View project.

**Why:** The owner corrected the standard-only removal as too narrow and approved the story-first recap for every existing and future project, including the recommendation to keep project budgets off Explore.

**How to apply:** Use a shared Explore presentation rather than project-specific exceptions. Keep complete story text and saved deal terms unchanged and accessible on project detail pages. Do not rewrite stored loglines or change private investor confirmation flows to implement this public browsing rule.
