---
name: Multi-project ownership boundaries
description: Why filmmaker account linking and project switching need separate proofs for identity and write context.
---

Multiple real filmmaker projects must remain accessible across devices, not just from the browser that submitted them. Keep guest-first intake, but do not claim a legacy submission based only on its typed contact email: require verified account identity and proof from the original browser. Do not silently replace an unfinished guest draft when the account has another unfinished draft.

**Why:** A typed email can belong to someone other than the submitter. Also, a visitor cookie is shared across browser tabs: after one tab selects a different project, a delayed save or upload from the first tab can otherwise alter the newly selected project.

**How to apply:** Keep earlier projects and public links intact. On any future account-linked write path, check both the verified owner and the specific project or draft the form was opened for; reject stale context rather than redirecting its write. Preserve guest data when ownership or draft-merge proof is insufficient.
