---
name: Showcase re-review boundary
description: Approved project content changes and their public-listing status.
---

Treat showcase approval as approval of the reviewed content, not a permanent project entitlement. Changing approved showcase details or media can return the project to review; submitting a worksheet or sharing its unlisted link does not request or grant showcase approval.

**Why:** Otherwise a filmmaker could replace reviewed public content while retaining a visible approval label, or the interface could claim the listing is still approved after a save that actually paused it.

**How to apply:** Warn before edits that may pause an approved listing, distinguish received submission from explicit review request and approval, and refresh or reconcile the authoritative project status after saves. Do not leave a locally cached approval label in control after a later successful status refresh.
