---
name: Filmmaker community count
description: The owner's counting rule and the anonymous identity boundary.
---

Count each filmmaker once overall, not once per milestone. First verified sign-in in the filmmaker journey, deliberate draft start/save, or completed filmmaker submission qualifies. Successful homepage header sign-in also qualifies immediately, without requiring a draft or opening My projects. Repeat saves/sign-ins, later submissions, and additional projects do not add another known filmmaker. Ordinary visitors and investor-flow sign-ins do not qualify by themselves.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected “Count each filmmaker once overall,” and subsequently confirmed that a new-email homepage header sign-in should increase the count in the preview.

**How to apply:** Preserve this rule when changing filmmaker entry points or the home-page community number. Connecting an original-browser guest draft to an account must reconcile the two identities through the existing secure claim, not create an additional filmmaker. Do not merge people by email or promise anonymous cross-browser deduplication. Once secure linkage proves two previously separate identities are the same account, the deduplicated total can reconcile downward; this is not a monotonically increasing activity counter.
