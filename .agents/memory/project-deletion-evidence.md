---
name: Project deletion evidence
description: Preserve signatures and payment evidence when removing owner-confirmed test projects.
---

A test-project label does not establish that attached signatures or payment records are disposable. Removing those projects must preserve account and historical financial evidence, without turning their old project allocations into unallocated general interest.

**Why:** The owner authorized deleting test projects, not purging investor identities, signed terms, or payment history. A paid checkout can also block physical project deletion unless its evidence is retained separately.

**How to apply:** Use a private historical archive where needed, retain original signed evidence unchanged, and preserve the original project linkage in that evidence. Do not null a project allocation and reinterpret it as new general interest, refund charges, or overwrite an entire live database to clear project listings.
