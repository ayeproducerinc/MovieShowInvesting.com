---
name: Proposal consent across onboarding changes
description: Why an unfinished legacy pitch must review new economics even when its saved repayment percentage is unchanged.
---

Keep completed submissions and signed investor records on their original economics. For an unfinished draft, preserve its answers and custom repayment input, but do not interpret an old acceptance choice as acceptance of newly introduced platform spreads or backend terms.

**Why:** The owner explicitly required no silent financial reinterpretation. Older drafts could record acceptance of a repayment amount without specifying backend percentages or duration; restoring them with new backend defaults would manufacture agreement.

**How to apply:** When a proposal version introduces or changes economics, require explicit review of the affected terms before final submission. Choosing the current standard terms is valid; merely restoring a previously accepted draft is not. Never infer unspecified backend terms for an already completed submission.
