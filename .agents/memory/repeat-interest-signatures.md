---
name: Repeat interest signatures
description: Why additional interest is separate from the initial signed investor record.
---

New non-binding interest after confirmation must remain an independent signed snapshot. Treat the original confirmed investor indication as historical evidence, including its original amount and allocations; never revise its signature or use it as a writable draft.

**Why:** Existing confirmed interest was originally stored on the investor's primary record. Reusing its writable fields for another indication would make the earlier signature appear to cover an amount or project the investor had not reviewed.

**How to apply:** When expanding account profiles, receipts, reporting, or owner notifications, distinguish the original confirmed indication from subsequent entries. Count only confirmed project allocations publicly, and make retries target the exact entry being signed.
