---
name: Public filmmaker credit consent
description: Keep private intake contact names separate from voluntarily published filmmaker credits.
---

Do not automatically publish or backfill a filmmaker's contact or authentication name when adding public project credits. Let the filmmaker explicitly choose a public credit in the existing pitch editor, including a blank value to remove it.

**Why:** The original intake said the contact information was shared with Movie Show Investing and was not a public listing. The owner requested a filmmaker credit near the project title, but that does not establish permission from historical submitters to publish their private contact names.

**How to apply:** Treat the public credit as project-specific content. Keep contact names, emails, phone numbers and authentication identities out of the public response. Retain the existing re-review rules for changes to approved public content.
