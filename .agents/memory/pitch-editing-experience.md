description: Owner-approved relationship between pitch details, materials editing, and explicit saves.
---

The owner approved putting Pitch materials near the top inside the existing Pitch details dropdown, while keeping a separate materials editor. Project-detail edits use an explicit Save pitch details action, with an unsaved-edit warning before switching to the materials editor.

**Why:** The owner said materials were lost at the bottom and approved this arrangement after asking how project-detail changes save. They accepted explicit saving and the warning rather than a new combined form or automatic detail saves.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction when changing the recap. Keep save feedback clear, retain edits if saving fails, and make the save-before-return path visibly return to the same project and its details section.
