---
name: International location fallback
description: Why international city suggestions are paired with manual entry in the filmmaker intake.
---

Keep a manual city, region, and country entry path alongside international city suggestions. The normal path should still require selecting a suggestion before treating the location as standardized.

**Why:** The owner chose international coverage, but the public place search can omit small settlements or become unavailable. Blocking the contact form in those cases would lose filmmaker submissions. Country remains a separate normalized code even for manual entry.

**How to apply:** When changing the provider, location validation, or the contact step, preserve a usable manual path and clearly distinguish selected suggestions from manually entered locations.
