---
name: Pay after pitch
description: Owner-approved ordering of filmmaker pitch creation and optional editorial-review payment
---

Both the free and $49 pricing choices lead to completing a pitch first. The $49 choice carries intent into a review offer after submission, not to an up-front checkout. The free route also offers the optional $49 review after completion. Opening the offer does not charge; only a verified payment for the completed pitch submits it to editorial review. No second confirmation is required after successful payment.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved this recommendation over pay-before-form, which could charge someone who never completes a pitch or leave an orphaned payment. Keeping the free unlisted result on cancellation makes the decision reversible without losing work.

**How to apply:** Preserve a single post-submission payment flow for both pricing routes. Keep the review offer visible even if checkout is temporarily unavailable, but never enable a payment action without verified account, price, pitch ownership, and prior payment status.
