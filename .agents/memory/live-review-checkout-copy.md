Keep the customer-facing live pitch-review checkout copy straightforward: state the $49 one-time fee, editorial review scope, no guaranteed approval or public listing, and refund/cancellation terms. Do not add repeated warnings that the card will be charged or that this is not a test payment. Do not mention testing environments in pricing copy or offer a test checkout on filmmaker-facing screens.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected conspicuous charge and testing-environment warnings as unnecessary for a live Stripe checkout and approved the simpler terms after reading the exact wording. The owner later explicitly requested that live payment become available, rather than remain disabled by an extra launch switch.

**How to apply:** When changing pricing, pre-checkout, or Stripe-hosted text, preserve the approved normal checkout tone; keep development-only testing internal and off the filmmaker-facing UI. Obtain separate approval for substantive review/refund wording changes. Never bypass the verified live account, price, and pitch-matching checks merely to make checkout appear available.
